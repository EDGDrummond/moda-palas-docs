# Pre-Demo Day

Before starting the 11-week process, we should organise an orientation day or 3-day kick-off event.

## Orientation-Kick off Event

### Day 1: Ignite

Introducing programme process and expectations by node101, team-building, and aligning on the vision for the next 11 weeks.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="handshake" title="Welcome & Vision Setting">
    - **Introduction Session:** Starting with a warm welcome from node101 and including mentors and partners.
    - **Programme Overview:** High-level breakdown of the 11-week process. Explain the focus on building, testing, and launching.
    - Interactive Q&A to address any initial questions or concerns.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="user-group" title="Founder Flash">
    Personal introductions—each founder shares their name, startup idea, and what they hope to achieve by Demo Day.

    #### Vision Setting Exercise
    Conduct a guided exercise where founders define their long-term vision, what success looks like for them, and their goals for the program.

    - **Pre-Exercise Setup:**
      - **Provide Vision Cards/Vision Board:** Distribute physical or digital "vision cards" with prompts like "In 5 years, our company will...," "The impact we want to create is...," or "The problem we solve at scale is..."

    - **Guided Vision Mapping (20-30 minutes):**
      - **Step 1: Vision Mapping Board:** Create a big visual board with areas labelled like *"Long-term Impact," "Team Goals," "Revenue Milestones," "Product Vision," and "Company Culture."* Founders place their vision statements in relevant areas.
      - **Step 2: Small Breakout Discussions:** Break participants into smaller groups (3-4 people) for peer-to-peer input and feedback.

    - **Creative Visual Representation (15 minutes):**
      - **Collaborative Mind Mapping:** Encourage founders to creatively express their visions through visual aids like mind maps or sketches.

    - **Personal Vision Pitch (10 minutes):**
      - **Pitch Your Vision:** Each founder does a quick 1-minute vision pitch, followed by brief feedback.

    - **Vision Alignment Discussion (10 minutes):**
      - Discuss standout visions, common themes, and how the programme can help achieve these visions.

    - **Wrap-Up with Collective Vision:**
      - Compile common themes into a **"Collective Vision"** for the cohort.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

#### Team Building Challenge

Each group will be paired with a specific mentor to their focus who provides feedback during the process. Each founder is assigned a specific role within the startup (CEO, CMO, etc.). This part should be meeting with mentors and founders, talking about the idea and setting goals for the next weeks.

#### Founders & Fables' Cocktail Mixer

- **Welcome & Drinks**
- **"Two Truths and a Startup Lie" Icebreaker**
  - Founders share **two truths** and **one made-up challenge**, and the group guesses the false one.
  - The group then votes on the most interesting truth and best "lie."
- **Dinner - Simone style**

### 🖇️ Application

- Team must exist
- After the application, they will evaluate you and face-to-face interviews begin
- You may need to provide research, additional presentations, or business validation. We expect these to be delivered quickly and with quality. If we believe this is the right programme for you, we'll invite you to Selection Day.

### ✳️ Selection Day

The QNBEYOND team and QNB Finansbank senior management jury will ask for a presentation of maximum 5 minutes. After this presentation, we'll complete our evaluation, and if you pass this stage, you'll officially be part of QNBEYOND!

### Accelerator Process

The programme runs for **6 months**.

### 🚬 Demo Day

At the end of the 6-month period, you'll have the opportunity to present to senior management at a bank-internal event.

You'll also have the chance to present your progress to an audience of important Turkish investors and ecosystem stakeholders.
